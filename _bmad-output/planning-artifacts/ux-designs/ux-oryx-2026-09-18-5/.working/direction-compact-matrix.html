Design direction 01

Compact Matrix

Compact Matrix treats Stock as a calm operational worksheet: thin rules, short controls, fixed numeric columns, and strong two-level headers make dense comparison feel deliberate rather than crowded.

Three grouping modes rearrange the same on-hand balances: by product, by warehouse, and by region-owned reserve. Owner and location type remain two cuts of one quantity and must not be added across groups.
