Oryx Stock · Design directions

Compare four investigation layouts

All variants preserve the same five views and factual-only data model; this page compares layout, density, and investigation flow.

Direction 01

Compact Matrix

Strength: Dense grouped columns maximize side-by-side product comparison while keeping stock state and location type distinct.

Tradeoff: The high information density demands more careful scanning and benefits most from a wide display.

Open full prototype

Direction 02

Split Inspector

Strength: A persistent facts panel keeps line-level evidence visible beside the demand table throughout an investigation.

Tradeoff: The three-column workspace gives the central table less horizontal room on smaller desktop screens.

Open full prototype

Direction 03

Pivot Workbench

Strength: The visible dimension strip makes row grain, measures, and scope explicit before quantities are compared.

Tradeoff: Grouped sections and dimension controls use more vertical space than a single compact matrix.

Open full prototype

Direction 04

Question Desk

Strength: Plain-language investigation prompts help operators enter the appropriate factual view and filter with less setup.

Tradeoff: The question row adds another navigation layer above the underlying tables and filters.
